    Questions

    I'm a total beginner. Is that OK?

    Yes. Your plan starts where you are and counts back from your move date.

    What time are the sessions?

    Evenings and weekends in Japan time, which is morning to afternoon in Europe and the Americas. The booking page shows your local time.

    I'm Muslim. Do you cover halal food and Ramadan?

    Yes. Reading labels, asking about pork and alcohol, and explaining fasting to coworkers. Pork and alcohol are everyday food here, so we prepare you to check, not to expect.

    Can you help with my visa?

    No. I only teach language and daily life. Please check visa questions with official sources.
